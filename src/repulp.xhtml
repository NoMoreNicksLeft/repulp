This Is a Replica

This ebook was not created by the original publisher. That work was printed on paper in an era before digital books were common or even possible. It has been digitally re-typeset by the Repulp Project and every effort has been made to ensure that it is accurate, complete, and authentic to the original.

It is not identical to the original printed edition. The limitations of printed works do not apply to EPUB format and therefor this work’s content has been streamlined to take advantage of that digital format’s features. You will find all stories, articles, and reports whole without interruption by notices of continuation on a later page. The text will “reflow” to fit the screen of your device and so there aren’t any permanent page numbers. The size and style of the text itself can be customized to accomodate your needs and preferences. Additionally, minor editorial adjustments may have been made to original errors when those might cause confusion. Occasionally magazines and newspapers will incorrectly print the volume and/or issue numbers; these ambiguities have been corrected when the publisher would also have done so had they noticed at the time of publication.

All advertisements and commercial content has been preserved. Advertisements are not embedded in the sequence of stories/articles so that they can be skipped or ignored as preferred. The EPUB format does not lend itself well to displaying multiple advertisements simultaneously; ads are presented in serial order. Authors, editors, and artists are credited in the EPUB’s internal metadata even when the original publisher chose not to do so.
