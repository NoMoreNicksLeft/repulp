A. Bertram Chandler, 1912–1984

A. Bertram Chandler, author of many stories appearing in Astounding and Analog between 1944 and 1970, died on June 6, 1984, following a heart attack at his home in Australia. He was perhaps best known for his “Rim Worlds” series, drawing on his own extensive experience as a merchant seaman, and for the much-anthologized “Giant Killer.”
