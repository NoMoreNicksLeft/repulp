Dr. Thomas C. Rainbow, 1954–1984

“Tom Rainbow” is a relatively new name to Analog readers. As I write this, that byline has appeared on only one story in these pages, but I knew Tom well enough to hope that there would be many more.

Most regrettably, there will not be. Tom (otherwise known as Dr. Thomas C. Rainbow, neuroscience researcher at the University of Pennsylvania) died in an accident near his home on September 6, 1984. He left several articles still to be published in a popular series in Isaac Asimov’s Science Fiction Magazine, and one or two stories may yet find their way to Analog. But beyond that, at least two fields have lost a very promising young man, and his readers will have had only the briefest glimpse of that promise.
