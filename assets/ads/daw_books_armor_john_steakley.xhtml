A novel of cosmic combat that will stand against the best of Gordon Dickson or Poul Anderson

The planet was called simply A-9, but it was the home world of mankind’s most implacable enemies. Body armor—giving a man the strengh of a 20th century tank force—would be worn by the commandos facing the hordes of antlike, almost unkillable beings.

But there’s more to war than battle. And what of the man within the weapon? What price his impregnable armor against an implacable foe? $3.95
